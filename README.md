# 🌾 AgriAI — Smart Agriculture Intelligence System

> **Advanced AI-Powered Precision Agriculture Platform Designed for Indian Farmers**  
> *Created & Developed by [Arjit Jaiswal](https://github.com/arjitjaiswal08-art)*

AgriAI is an intelligent, accessible web application engineered to optimize farming decisions, detect crop diseases, provide micro-climate weather advisories, deliver real-time mandi price intelligence, and formulate cost-effective agronomic recommendations for Indian farmers across 42 crop varieties and 13 regional languages.

🌐 **Live Demo**: [agri-intelligence-system.vercel.app](https://agri-intelligence-system.vercel.app)

## 🌟 Key Features

- 📸 **Crop Disease Detection & Plant Vision AI**: Instant leaf photo visual diagnosis + symptom matcher across 19+ crop diseases with low-cost organic & chemical treatment protocols.
- 🌾 **42 Indian Crop Varieties Supported**: Full coverage across Cereals & Millets, Pulses & Legumes, Oilseeds, Commercial & Cash Crops, Vegetables & Spices, and Fruits & Plantation.
- 🌦️ **Micro-Climate Weather Advisor**: 5-day agro-meteorological forecasting with spray-safe windows, irrigation scheduling, and extreme weather alerts.
- 📈 **Mandi Price & Market Analyst**: Real-time wholesale APMC mandi benchmark tracking, MSP price data, seasonal cycles, and strategic sell vs. hold verdicts.
- 🌱 **Fertilizer & Soil Planner**: Balanced N-P-K dosage calculators adjusted for soil type, bio-fertilizer schedules, and PM-PRANAM scheme linkages.
- 🔄 **End-to-End Smart Decision Engine**: Unified 5-step pipeline that synthesizes leaf imagery, local weather, and market trends into a tactical 48-hour farming plan.
- 🎙️ **Kisan Vaani (किसान वाणी / কৃষক বাণী) — Multilingual Voice Assistant**:
  - **13 Regional Languages & Dialects**:
    - **North East India**: Assamese (অসমীয়া), Bodo (बड़ो), Manipuri (মৈতৈলোন্), Khasi (Ka Ktien Khasi), Mizo (Mizo ṭawng), Nepali (नेपाली), Nagamese.
    - **Uttar Pradesh & Bihar**: Bhojpuri (भोजपुरी), Maithili (मैथिली), Magahi (मगही), Awadhi (अवधी).
    - **National & Southern**: Hindi (हिन्दी), English, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Punjabi, Odia.
  - **100% Autonomous & Key-Free**: Native browser Web Speech API (`SpeechRecognition` & `SpeechSynthesis`).
  - **Animated Multi-Frequency Equalizer**: 12-bar real-time soundwave visualizer.
  - **Vernacular Spoken Advisory**: Reads comprehensive diagnostic and market reports aloud in the farmer's dialect.

## 📖 How to Use AgriAI

1. **Set Up Your Farm Profile**:
   - Open the **Dashboard** and click **Edit Profile**.
   - Select your State, District, primary crop, growth stage, and soil type.
   - Your profile automatically pre-populates all AI modules!

2. **Detect Crop Diseases (Plant Vision AI)**:
   - Navigate to **Disease Detector** or **End-to-End Flow**.
   - Snap a picture of an affected leaf using your phone camera or upload an image.
   - The visual engine scans color distributions and patterns, identifies the pathogen, and provides organic & chemical control measures.

3. **Check Weather & Spray Windows**:
   - Navigate to **Weather Advisor**.
   - Input current rainfall, temperature, and wind conditions (or use live geolocation).
   - Get safe spraying windows, irrigation advice, and humidity risk forecasts.

4. **Analyze Mandi Prices & Selling Strategy**:
   - Navigate to **Market Analyst**.
   - Select your crop, state, and storage availability.
   - Check current mandi rates against government MSP, view 6-month price seasonality, and receive a clear **Sell Now** or **Hold in Cold Storage** recommendation.

5. **Calculate Customized Fertilizer Doses**:
   - Navigate to **Fertilizer Planner**.
   - Select your crop, current stage, and field soil type (Alluvial, Black, Red, Sandy, etc.).
   - Receive tailored Urea, DAP, and MOP quantities alongside organic manure and bio-stimulant schedules.

6. **Run Full AI Decision Pipeline**:
   - Navigate to **End-to-End Flow**.
   - Click **Run Pipeline** for a 5-second complete diagnostic and operational plan for your farm.

7. **Voice Assistant (Kisan Vaani)**:
   - Tap the **🎙️ Kisan Vaani** floating orb or header button.
   - Choose your regional language/dialect and speak or listen to real-time farming intelligence hands-free!

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend & Architecture**: HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), Vanilla CSS3 (Custom Dark Forest Glassmorphism Theme)
- **Design System**: Responsive mobile-first design, Google Fonts (Outfit & Inter), Glassmorphism backdrop filters
- **AI Diagnostics**: Heuristic expert decision trees, offline static agronomic knowledge engines
- **Computer Vision**: HTML5 Canvas pixel analysis & HSV color distribution classifier
- **Voice Intelligence**: W3C Web Speech API (`SpeechRecognition`, `SpeechSynthesis`) with regional BCP-47 locale mapping
- **Weather Integration**: OpenWeatherMap API with automatic fallback simulation
- **Deployment**: Vercel Edge Network with instant global distribution
- **Zero Heavy Frameworks**: 100% autonomous client-side execution, instant load times on 2G/3G rural networks
